Why Remote Engineers Are Trading Lonely 1BHK Flats for Architectural Co-Living

By Aarav Mehta & Meera Krishnan • Published November 14, 2026 • 6 Min Read

Table of Contents

1. The Hidden Tax of Working From the Same Room You Sleep In

When distributed work became permanent for thousands of software engineers and product designers, the initial freedom felt intoxicating. Yet within six months of renting a standalone 1BHK flat, a familiar pattern emerges: waking up, walking four feet from bed to laptop, ordering lunch on an app, and realizing at 9 PM that you have not spoken a single sentence out loud all day.

2. Why Architectural Separation Matters for Deep Work

At Nestly, we designed our houses so your bedroom remains a calm, screen-optional sanctuary while Level 2 acts as a true engineering studio—complete with 27-inch 4K monitors, acoustic phone booths, and peers who understand why your deploy pipeline matters.

3. Escaping the 10-Month Security Deposit Lock-In

By bundling enterprise dual-ISP fiber, DG backup, bi-weekly housekeeping, and water/power into one predictable invoice—backed by a 1-month refundable deposit—young professionals keep their liquidity invested while upgrading their daily quality of life.
