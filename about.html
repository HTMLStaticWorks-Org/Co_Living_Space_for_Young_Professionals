Our Founding Story

We started Nestly after moving to a new city and hating both options.

In 2022, our co-founders Meera and Arjun relocated to Bengaluru. Option A was a sterile, curfew-policed PG with fluorescent tube lights. Option B was a lonely unfurnished flat demanding 10 months of security deposit. Nestly was born to create Option C: warm residential architecture where young adults can live with dignity, autonomy, and friendship.

Planet-Positive Homes

70% solar-powered houses with zero single-use plastics.

Every Nestly house retrofits existing urban buildings rather than demolishing them—cutting embodied carbon by 62%. We run rooftop solar arrays, greywater recycling for terrace gardens, and glass-bottle RO refill stations on every floor.
